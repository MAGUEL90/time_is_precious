Status: **implementation for Game Director review**. Baseline: `9f80d10fe674d62c68565dbd8099404a9da8dd1d`. Branch: `feature/gameplay/finite-sites-hauler-review-20261104`. The starting tree was clean. ## Changes

- The physical City Storage footer says **Send** and opens the existing city-storage-to-city-storage shipment panel. The Job Board offers Haulers and describes that duty. A new game keeps Laborers Naram and Lugal and adds Hauler Belum as a third resident/applicant. All use the existing paid contracts and satisfaction threshold; hauling still requires a cart. The additional resident also creates normal food, clothing and shelter needs; this balance effect is intentional for review. Worksites continue to reject new Hauler assignments.
- When an older checkpoint has no resident Hauler, loading adds one stable-ID Belum applicant at the same 55% starter satisfaction. It does not convert an existing Laborer or change their XP, contract or wallet, and the original save file stays untouched until the next explicit save. Repeated loads do not duplicate the resident. An existing resident Hauler prevents the extra applicant, and a previously departed Belum is not silently recreated. For a checkpoint already at the collection or file-size limit, the addition is skipped so that the existing save remains loadable. The added resident has normal needs; this one-time population change is explicit for review.
- The former palm sine wave made one right and one left excursion every five seconds, with a maximum of two source pixels on each side of center. It now still makes one excursion per side every five seconds but reaches one pixel per side. The shader samples the canopy and upper neck; the lower half of the trunk stays fixed. The one-pixel interpretation of “reduce one movement” is explicit for review. The pre-existing footstep animation remains.
- The five main-map worksite deposits (clay, wood, reed, straw and water) start with **240 units each**, a temporary balance value for review. At clay's 10 minutes per unit this is five ideal eight-hour shifts for one worker before travel and satisfaction modifiers. Hourly player gathering and Daily worker gathering consume the same deposit. Hourly output remains player property; Daily output goes to City Storage. A deposit no longer refills at midnight.
- Once a deposit is exhausted and its current transaction finishes, the replacement is selected from bounded offsets around the original marker. Its sampled footprint and paths must be on the authored ground and clear of the current collision geometry, authored property plots, player and visible workers. Assignments remain, but workers must physically reach the new site before producing. Search is bounded to avoid hanging a frame. If no candidate can be verified, the deposit remains exhausted and retries after 60 game minutes; it is not refilled at the old position. Relocation waits while paused or while the city is parked during a home visit. This prototype uses a finite candidate set, not unconstrained procedural placement. Date palms and unrelated pickup sources are outside these five deposits.
- Worksite snapshot schema **4 → 5** records a validated offset and remaining stock. The unchanged authored position is the implicit default, so migration does not add five redundant offsets to a near-limit v4 save. Older snapshots keep their saved stock and begin at the original authored marker; loading does not refill them. Existing v4 city-owned pending output remains city-owned, whereas pre-v4 pending ground output keeps its previous migration behavior. New version-5 worksite snapshots are not readable by the older `9f80d10` executable; preserve a backup if downgrading.
