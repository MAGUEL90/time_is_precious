## Result

The cart resource repair, a narrowly scoped cart-clothing resolver improvement, and the schema-18 HOME research tests are combined in one frozen cloud candidate. **78 selected final headless executions passed their expected outcomes**: 55 broader regressions, 14 HOME/neighbor stages and nine focused checks. Three executions intentionally exit nonzero to test failure/unsafe-profile guards. This is not graphical, Android or whole-project acceptance.

The independent genuine schema-17 HOME research also passed 552 assertions in 17 normal processes plus two expected guard refusals, using a **test-only byte-preserved copy**. It does not complete a production save migration.

The candidate is `project/`, on branch `test/save-load/cloud-followup-combined`. It contains 3,368 non-cache files. Compared with the frozen integrated asset baseline, exactly three production files changed:

- `scenes/worker_visual/worker_cart.tscn`: two texture references now use the supplied `tools_assets` directory.

The resolver accepts only IDs already present in the clothing catalog, valid identifiers and known cart actions. Default, invalid, unknown, missing and malformed art keeps the existing hidden-layer fallback. No new outfit is registered and no profile, identity, save, frame timing, layer order, gameplay rule or artwork is changed. Real `plain_worn_wrap` cart sheets are still absent. Test-only in-memory sheets prove future matching art can be recognized; they are not shipped as artwork.

Two test scripts were added for isolated authored-palm HOME research. They inject a synthetic authored palm and prebound city only in tests. No authored user map or production palm startup hook was modified. All other existing files are byte-identical, with no deletions. Fifteen editor-generated UID sidecars are preserved outside the source candidate. No laptop or real save was opened, and there was no commit, merge, push or deployment.

See `../palm_home_genuine17_cloud_20261109/REVIEW.md` and its `qa/final_byte_preserved/manifest.json`. The genuine unchanged schema-17 writer produced HOME checkpoints.
